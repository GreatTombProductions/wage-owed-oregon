# Wage Owed Oregon

A static Oregon wage-hour calculator focused on tipped workers. Oregon does not allow tip credits: tips are the worker's property and cannot count toward hourly minimum wage.

**Live site:** https://GreatTombProductions.github.io/wage-owed-oregon/

## What this is

Estimate Oregon minimum wage, overtime, unpaid worked time, and late final paycheck exposure for a workweek or repeated weekly pattern. Covers Portland Metro, Standard, and Nonurban Oregon rates with BOLI source links.

## What this isn't

- Not legal advice
- Not a substitute for BOLI, an attorney, or a worker center
- Not a backend or user platform
- Not federal tip-credit logic — Oregon rules are stricter
