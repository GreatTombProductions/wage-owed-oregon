Tips are yours.

Oregon does not allow tip credits. Your employer cannot count your tips against minimum wage. Estimate what you may be owed for minimum wage, overtime, worked-through breaks, and late final pay.

Core rule: Oregon BOLI states: “Tip credits are illegal in Oregon. Your tips are yours and cannot be counted against your hourly pay.” This calculator treats tips as separate from wages paid by the employer.

What this calculator assumes
  • You are non-exempt from overtime.
  • Overtime applies after 40 hours actually worked in a workweek.
  • Tips do not reduce what your employer owes in hourly wages.
  • Worked-through unpaid meal periods and unpaid rest breaks are work time.

Oregon rules embedded here

Minimum wage regions

  • Portland Metro: $16.30/hour
  • Standard: $15.05/hour
  • Nonurban: $14.05/hour

Breaks and paychecks

  • Rest breaks are paid work time.
  • If you work through an unpaid meal period, that time must be paid.
  • If fired, final paycheck is generally due by the end of the next business day.
