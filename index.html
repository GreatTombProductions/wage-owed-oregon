Oregon unpaid wages

Get your hours and pay ready for an Oregon wage claim

Enter the days you worked as well as you remember them. This page builds the day-by-day hours record that Oregon’s Bureau of Labor and Industries (BOLI) asks wage claimants to upload, works out what you may be owed where it safely can, and lays out the online form’s wage questions in order. Nothing you enter leaves this browser.

Tips are not part of these figures. Oregon does not allow tip credits: tips cannot be counted against your hourly pay, so they are left out of every figure here.

Keep your work
